**What this ticket can now use from cellb (`main`, not yet published)**
- Fragment host on Node: `createFragmentHost`. In the browser: `joinFragmentShards`, `serveFragmentShard`, and `installProjectRouter` in the ServiceWorker. These serve `/fragments/{id}/…` to the fragment DO (#191's work).
- Bindings: `services: [{ binding, service: "<fragment-id>", entrypoint?, props? }]` in a fragment's `wrangler.jsonc` binds another fragment held by the same host. The call runs on the caller's shard or through the router on another shard. Contracts are cloneable values plus at most one stream; anything else fails with a `DataCloneError` that names the call. A fragment that isn't active fails with `ServiceUnavailableError`.
- Cross-host: `createFragmentHost({ routes, remote, peerId })` and `FragmentHost.serveLink` support `/peers/{peerId}/fragments/{id}/`.
