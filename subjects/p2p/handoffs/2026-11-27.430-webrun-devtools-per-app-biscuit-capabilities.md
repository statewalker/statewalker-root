## Rules the next session needs

- **Order of steps 1 and 2.** Merge webrun-wire#223 only after the first publish. Re-running the httpeers publish job needs no code change and no new pull request.
- **Redesign shape.** Once §9 is answered, run `/to-spec` and then `/to-tickets` here. The tickets go lower layers first (webrun-wire, then httpeers, then webrun-devtools), and webrun-biscuit's move to webrun-capabilities ([#98](https://github.com/statewalker/statewalker-root/issues/98)) may come first.
- **Design facts settled by the prototypes.** Their notes are linked from §10 of the analysis:
  - the offscreen document hosts hubs and members;
  - tab ownership comes from `webNavigation`, not `openerTabId`;
  - origin conditions go in the broker's authorizer, not as token checks;
  - one libp2p node per mesh;
  - mesh roles map to rights per trusted mesh.
- **Hub self-calls.** Pages built on the demos' hub pattern send their self-calls through `asHub()` ([httpeers#172](https://github.com/statewalker/httpeers/pull/172)).
