## Done

- All three steps of #235 merged, through the merge gate, the last as
  [cellb.dev#240](https://github.com/statewalker/cellb.dev/pull/240):
  - themed notebook pages run TypeScript, JSX and TSX cells;
  - each page script carries a source map back to its `.md` or `.html` notebook (magic-string and
    `@jridgewell/remapping` over notebook-build's per-cell maps);
  - a throwing cell shows its notebook position on the page (`features.md:58:9` in the demo, on
    CellB, Wrangler and celld), from a compile-time table, so no source-map library loads.
- notebook-build released 0.4.0 (JSX/TSX cells) and 0.5.0 (per-cell source maps), through CI.
- The review's two high findings were fixed in #240: unmapped cells now leave nothing in the map
  and show no position (multi-line Kit Markdown expressions are mapped); maps keep one entry per
  unchanged stretch, so the error table fell from 869 KB to 7 KB on a 200-cell notebook.
- Tests: 50 unit tests in `packages/notebooks`; the notebooks end-to-end checks pass 11/11 on all
  three hosts.

## Decisions to keep
